## The camera no longer follows the founder

The owner: "I don't want the camera to follow the founder." `main.ts`'s ticker eased the camera to `snap.hero ??
snap.mainId`: the person the player chose to follow, else the founder. It now follows only `snap.hero` (the Follow
action on a person's tap card); with nobody followed the camera stays where the player leaves it. The founder stands
out instead with a marker over the head (docs/notes/raid-looks.md).
