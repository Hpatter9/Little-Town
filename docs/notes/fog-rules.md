## Fog that matters (building, scouts, raids)

Builds on "Fog of war" (`docs/notes/fog-of-war.md`, `sim/fog.ts`). Three rules, each only where the fog has started (`s.explored` is set; an older save with none behaves as before until its first fog tick).

**1. Building only on explored land.** `canPlace` (`sim/buildings.ts`) refuses a footprint with a cell not in `s.explored` ("Explore further to make room", `UNEXPLORED`); everything that builds goes through it (the player's `placeBuilding`, pins, fields, pens, the ring's pieces). The spot searches skip fogged cells so they keep looking rather than fail on the best spot: `flatSpot` (`ok` of `spiralSpot`), `rockSpot`, and `layoutSpot` (`bad` and the door check in `sim/layout.ts`). `rectExplored` in `fog.ts` is the shared test. The ring wall is not laid or widened unless its whole line is explored (`lineSeen` in `sim/ringWall.ts`, which sets `fogNeed`); `planStreets` and the gate paths only pave explored cells. A pin the planner can't place in the fog shows "waiting: explore further to make room" in the build menu (`p.why`), and a refused `placeBuilding` now says why in the news. The placement ghost uses `snap.explored`.

**2. Scouts worth more** (`sim/explore.ts`, numbers in `data/sight.ts`). `fogEdge(s)`: explored, walkable, open cells that border the unexplored, within `exploreReach` (town radius + `EXPLORE_BEYOND` 18, never past `land.open`); cached by the `explored` string. An explorer is only a `wander` task marked `explore: true`: the fog lifts where they walk and the land's dangers apply. `scoutTask` (called in `people.ts` `updatePerson` when a person has nothing to do; autopilot only, by day, grown-ups who are fed, rested and well, never the founder): `SCOUT_CLASSES` (ranger, hunter) always, anyone idle while `fogNeed` is recent (`FOG_NEED_HOURS` 36; the planner sets `GameState.fogNeed` when it found no room and fog remains, or a ring waits on unseen land). At most `EXPLORE_QUOTA` 1 + a grown-ups / `EXPLORE_PER_PEOPLE` 10 are out at once. The target is one of the 10 nearest edge cells no other explorer is bound for, picked from a side stream `hashSeed(`${seed}:explore:${tick}:${id}`)`.
**The founder:** command `founderExplore { dir 0..7 | null }` (`sim/founder.ts`, `FounderOrder` kind `explore`): a day's exploring toward a wind, edge to edge, ending with a note when that way is all seen within reach. The Founder's Orders sheet has the eight winds (greyed when nothing is left that way, `FounderView.winds`).

**3. Raids hidden until spotted** (`sim/raidSight.ts`, `data/raidSight.ts`). Only the scheduled land raid (`maybeStartRaid`; `startRaid(..., veiled = true)`, autopilot on and fog started) sets out veiled: `Raid.veil { from, total, flank }`, a vague sign in the news (`RAID_SIGNS`), no prompt, no warning, and `snapshot().raid` is null. Its approach is virtual: along the camp's row, `APPROACH_CELLS` 40 past the town's edge, closing to 0 at `arrivesTick`. Every 5 ticks `updateRaid` asks `raidSeenBy`: a townsperson within `sightOf`, or a finished building within `buildingSight` (new, in `fog.ts`: 4, a wall piece 2, a watchtower, any gate or the seat 12). On the first sighting the warning is raised as before (`announceRaid`, the old prompt code moved out of `startRaid`), with at least `SPOTTED_LEAD_TICKS` 50 to answer. A raid nobody sees simply arrives (veil cleared at `arrivesTick`). Everything after the warning is untouched: raids wait for you, the pause on pop-ups, skip, the tactics board. The blessing (`religionTick`), rites (`magic`), tribute (`tradeEmpire`) and Sea Fog (`powers`) that react to a raid's warning wait until it is seen.

Tests: `test/fogRules.test.ts`.

### For the owner
- Other raid sources (war hosts, sagas, Great Enemy, sea raids, raids from inside the town) still warn as before; only the ordinary scheduled land raid is hidden. Say if the rest should follow.
- A well-watched town (towers, gates) gets its warning about as early as before; an unwatched one gets about 5 seconds (`SPOTTED_LEAD_TICKS`), so the first raids are the surprise the owner asked for. The number is easy to raise.
- "No room" is treated as "maybe the fog": when the planner is blocked and fog remains, any idle adult may go and look (not only rangers and hunters). Without it a town with no ranger would be stuck.
- The explored area grows only by sight (walking, buildings, towers). The founder's explore order and idle scouts stop at the town's reach plus 18 cells; further than that needs the town to grow.
