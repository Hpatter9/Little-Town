# Choice events (planned)

Events that stop and ask the player, picked at random now and then (see CLAUDE.md, "Choices that wait for you").
Each waits a while (a few real hours at most), then the **default** (marked *) is taken, so the town never stalls.
Tags: when it can happen (an era, an origin, a building or state it needs). Nothing here is built yet.

## Strangers and newcomers

1. **A stranger at the gate** asks to join. *Take them in* / Turn them away*. (A wanderer with a secret trait.)
2. **A family of refugees**, four of them, fleeing a war. *Take them all in* (food strain) / Take the children only / Send them on*.
3. **A sick traveller** begs shelter. *Shelter them* (plague risk; grateful gift) / Turn them away*.
4. **A deserter from a rival army** (rival origins). *Hide them* (a fighter; their lord may come looking) / Hand them back* (renown with that lord).
5. **A child found in the woods**. *Raise them* / Leave them at the shrine* (morale hit either way, a little).
6. **A famous craftsman** passing through asks for room and board for a week. *Host them* (crafting quality up; costs food) / Decline*.
7. **A bard** offers to stay the winter. *Let them* (morale all winter; eats) / Pay them to move on*.
8. **A hermit** comes down from the hills with a strange tome. *Take the tome* (reveals the Occult) / Send them away*.
9. **A disgraced knight** asks to swear to the founder. *Accept* (a strong fighter, a quarrelsome one) / Refuse*.
10. **Twins born**: name one after a hero of the town. *Name them* (morale) / Let the parents choose* (same, smaller).
11. **A mail-order bride/groom arrives** for a townsperson who never asked. *Let them stay* / Send them home*.
12. **A runaway apprentice** from another town, their master close behind. *Hide them* / Hand them back* (coins).

## Raiders, rivals and war

13. **A raider chief offers terms**: tribute now, or they attack. *Pay* (half the coins or stores) / Fight*.
14. **Scouts spotted** on the ridge. *Ambush them* (fighters go; a fight on your terms) / Let them pass* (a raid sooner).
15. **A rival lord sends an envoy** (rival origins). *Accept a truce* (no raids from them for a season; tribute) / Refuse*.
16. **Captured raider** offers to lead you to their camp. *Follow* (an expedition to loot it) / Keep them prisoner*.
17. **A mercenary band** offers its swords for a season. *Hire them* (coins; strong defenders) / Decline*.
18. **Hunters' Guild** wants to search the town for monsters (vampire, werewolf or lich towns). *Let them* (risky) / Bar the gates* (a raid).
19. **A beaten raider pleads for mercy** after a raid. *Spare them* (a prisoner or a recruit) / Execute them* (renown).
20. **War drums in the distance**: an army is passing through. *Hide in the walls* (work stops a day) / Trade with them* (coins; some risk).
21. **A spy is caught** in the stores. *Question them* (the next raid's timing) / Hang them* / Let them go (a favour later).
22. **The watchman fell asleep** and raiders nearly got in. *Punish them* / Forgive them* (morale).
23. **A rival's relic** turns up in a traveller's pack. *Buy it* (coins; it may draw that lord) / Leave it*.
24. **The Horde's riders** offer a horse trade. *Trade* (horses for stores) / Refuse*.

## Plague, hunger and weather

25. **A fever spreads** in one home. *Quarantine it* (they may die; the rest are safe) / Nurse them together* (risk).
26. **The well is foul**. *Dig a new one* (work and stone) / Boil the water* (slower work a few days).
27. **Early frost** threatens the harvest. *Harvest now* (smaller yield) / Wait and hope* (all or nothing).
28. **Locusts** on the horizon. *Burn the edge fields* (lose some, save the rest) / Pray* (random).
29. **Flood**: the river rises. *Save the fields* / Save the stores* (one is damaged).
30. **Wildfire** in the forest. *Cut a firebreak* (lose some forest) / Let it burn out* (it may reach the town).
31. **A hard winter** is coming, the elders say. *Ration food now* (morale down, fewer deaths) / Eat as usual*.
32. **Drought**: *Carry water from the river* (work) / Let the fields rest* (no harvest this season).
33. **A bumper crop**: *Feast* (morale) / Store it all* / Sell it to travellers (coins).
34. **Rats in the granary**. *Get cats* (need a trader) / Poison* (a small risk to people) / Burn the granary.
35. **A plague ship** (coast) asks to land. *Let them* (trade; plague risk) / Wave them off*.
36. **Poisoned berries** made three people sick. *Burn the bushes* / Mark them and move on*.

## Faith, omens and the strange

37. **A comet** in the sky. *Hold a ritual* (herbs; morale) / Ignore it* / Call it a bad sign (the town prepares: defence up, morale down).
38. **An eclipse**. *Hide indoors* (work stops) / Watch it* (research boost, a little fear).
39. **A holy wanderer** asks to build a shrine. *Let them* (morale building, costs stone) / Refuse*.
40. **The dead walk** in the graveyard one night. *Burn the graves* (grief) / Post a guard* / Ask the necromancer (necromancer).
41. **A talking animal** at the edge of the woods (druid, fae). *Follow it* (an expedition) / Shoo it away*.
42. **A fairy ring** appears in a field (fae, forest). *Dance in it* (random: blessing or curse) / Mow it down*.
43. **A voice in the well** offers a wish. *Wish for gold* / Wish for health / Seal the well*.
44. **A meteor falls** nearby. *Dig it out* (rare minerals; risky) / Leave it*.
45. **Blood moon** (vampire, werewolf). *Hold the hunt* (fight boost; someone may be hurt) / Lock the doors*.
46. **A phylactery's whisper** (lich). *Feed it a soul* (a townsperson; power) / Starve it* (the lich weakens a while).
47. **A ghost** haunts a home. *Exorcise it* (herbs) / Live with it* (morale down there) / Speak with it (a secret).
48. **Strange lights** over the marsh. *Investigate* (someone goes; could vanish) / Stay away*.
49. **The founder dreams** of a place far away. *Send scouts* (a new destination) / Forget it*.
50. **A statue** unearthed while digging. *Set it up in the square* (morale) / Sell it* (coins) / Smash it (a curse lifted, or not).

## Trade and coin

51. **A merchant offers a rare relic** for most of the coins. *Buy it* / Pass*.
52. **A caravan's axle broke** outside town. *Help them* (work; a gift) / Charge them* (coins) / Ignore them.
53. **A moneylender** offers a loan. *Borrow* (coins now, more back later) / Refuse*.
54. **Counterfeit coins** found in the till. *Hunt the forger* (a fight) / Swallow the loss*.
55. **A noble wants to buy the town's best item**. *Sell* (lots of coins) / Keep it*.
56. **A traveller's dog** won't leave. *Keep it* (a guard dog; eats) / Shoo it*.
57. **The guild offers a contract**: send three people away for two days for a big reward. *Send them* / Decline*.
58. **Tax collector** from a far kingdom (Medieval+). *Pay* / Refuse* (a small raid later) / Bribe them.
59. **A rich traveller dies** at the tavern. *Keep the purse* / Send it to their family* (renown).
60. **A market fair** asks to set up in town. *Host it* (coins, crowds, some theft) / Decline*.
61. **Smugglers** want to store goods in the cellars. *Let them* (coins; raids more likely) / Refuse*.
62. **A caravan offers exotic seeds**. *Buy* (a new crop grows faster for a season) / Pass*.

## Townsfolk and their lives

63. **Two townsfolk quarrel** over a spouse, a debt, a tool. *Side with one* / Side with the other / Let them sort it out* (a fight may break out).
64. **A townsperson wants to leave** for the city. *Let them go* / Ask them to stay* (they may sulk).
65. **A marriage proposal** between townsfolk of rival jobs. *Bless it* / Forbid it* (both sulk).
66. **A thief among you**: stores keep going missing. *Search the homes* (morale down) / Set a trap* (a few days).
67. **An elder is dying** and asks to see the sea (or the mountains). *Send them with an escort* / Keep them comfortable*.
68. **A child's prank** set a fire. *Punish them* / Laugh it off* (another prank later).
69. **The best hunter** wants to go after the beast that killed their brother. *Let them* (an expedition alone) / Forbid it*.
70. **A duel for the founder's honour**. *Accept* (the founder or a champion fights) / Decline* (morale down a little).
71. **A townsperson confesses** to a past crime. *Forgive* / Exile them*.
72. **A drunk brawl** at the tavern. *Throw them in the stocks* / Let it go*.
73. **Someone wants to build their own home** away from town. *Let them* (a lone house, raids hit it first) / No*.
74. **A townsperson has a talent** (music, carving). *Give them time off to practise* (a new ware later) / Keep them working*.
75. **A funeral**: *Hold a great wake* (food; grief passes faster) / Bury them quietly*.
76. **A birthday of the founder**. *Hold a feast* (food; morale) / Work as usual*.
77. **An apprentice** wants to learn a trade from a master. *Let them* (skill grows fast, both slower at work) / Not now*.
78. **The midwife** needs herbs no one has. *Send someone to the hills* (an expedition) / Make do*.

## Land, ruins and beasts

79. **An old ruin** found nearby. *Dig now* (loot; it may wake something) / Seal it* / Study it (research).
80. **A monster's lair** found. *Hunt it* (a boss fight, a trophy) / Leave it be*.
81. **A cave** opens after a quake. *Explore* (ore; danger) / Block it*.
82. **Wolves** have been taking the livestock. *Hunt the pack* / Build a better fence* (wood).
83. **A bear** sleeps under the stockpile. *Drive it off* (a fight) / Let it sleep* (the pile is off limits a day).
84. **A giant tree** falls across the road. *Cut it up* (lots of wood; a day's work) / Go around it* (slower hauling).
85. **A spring of hot water** found. *Build a bathhouse* (morale; stone) / Leave it*.
86. **A sinkhole** swallows a field. *Fill it* (stone) / Make it a pond* (fish).
87. **Wild horses** in the meadow. *Catch them* (horses; someone may get hurt) / Let them run*.
88. **Bees** in the old oak. *Take the honey* (stings) / Leave them* (the fields grow a little better).
89. **A dragon** is seen over the mountains (rare). *Hide the gold* / Offer tribute / Ignore it* (maybe it comes).
90. **Bandit trail** found. *Follow it* (loot a hideout) / Report it to travellers* (renown).

## Era and origin moments

91. **The first printing press** (Medieval). *Print pamphlets* (research up, unrest) / Print scripture* (morale).
92. **Railway surveyors** want to lay a line through (Industrial). *Allow it* (travellers up, a field lost) / Refuse*.
93. **A factory strike** (Industrial). *Raise wages* (coins) / Wait it out* (no crafting two days).
94. **A radio broadcast** calls for volunteers (Modern). *Send them* (renown, lose people a while) / Stay home*.
95. **A drone crash** in the square (Space). *Salvage it* (circuits) / Return it* (coins).
96. **The machines ask for names** (robot). *Name them* (spirits up) / Keep serial numbers*.
97. **The grove asks for a sacrifice** (druid). *Give a tree-felling tool* / Give nothing* (the forest grows back faster).
98. **The court demands a ball** (vampire). *Hold it* (coins spent; prices up a season) / Cancel it* (morale down).
99. **The pack wants to hunt the moon** (werewolf). *Let them run* (fight up; someone lost) / Chain the gates*.
100. **The tribe splits over the route** (nomads, before settling). *Go by the river* (slow, safe) / Over the pass* (fast, raiders).
