# Choice events (planned)

Events that stop and ask the player, picked at random now and then (see CLAUDE.md, "Choices that wait for you").
Each waits a while (a few real hours at most), then the **default** is taken, so the town never stalls.
Tags: when it can happen (an era, an origin, a building or state it needs). Nothing here is built yet.

## Strangers and newcomers

1. **A stranger at the gate** asks to join. Take them in / Turn them away (default). (A wanderer with a secret trait.)
2. **A family of refugees**, four of them, fleeing a war. Take them all in (food strain) / Take the children only / Send them on (default).
3. **A sick traveller** begs shelter. Shelter them (plague risk; grateful gift) / Turn them away (default).
4. **A deserter from a rival army** (rival origins). Hide them (a fighter; their lord may come looking) / Hand them back (default) (renown with that lord).
5. **A child found in the woods**. Raise them / Leave them at the shrine (default) (morale hit either way, a little).
6. **A famous craftsman** passing through asks for room and board for a week. Host them (crafting quality up; costs food) / Decline (default).
7. **A bard** offers to stay the winter. Let them (morale all winter; eats) / Pay them to move on (default).
8. **A hermit** comes down from the hills with a strange tome. Take the tome (reveals the Occult) / Send them away (default).
9. **A disgraced knight** asks to swear to the founder. Accept (a strong fighter, a quarrelsome one) / Refuse (default).
10. **Twins born**: name one after a hero of the town. Name them (morale) / Let the parents choose (default) (same, smaller).
11. **A mail-order bride/groom arrives** for a townsperson who never asked. Let them stay / Send them home (default).
12. **A runaway apprentice** from another town, their master close behind. Hide them / Hand them back (default) (coins).

## Raiders, rivals and war

13. **A raider chief offers terms**: tribute now, or they attack. Pay (half the coins or stores) / Fight (default).
14. **Scouts spotted** on the ridge. Ambush them (fighters go; a fight on your terms) / Let them pass (default) (a raid sooner).
15. **A rival lord sends an envoy** (rival origins). Accept a truce (no raids from them for a season; tribute) / Refuse (default).
16. **Captured raider** offers to lead you to their camp. Follow (an expedition to loot it) / Keep them prisoner (default).
17. **A mercenary band** offers its swords for a season. Hire them (coins; strong defenders) / Decline (default).
18. **Hunters' Guild** wants to search the town for monsters (vampire, werewolf or lich towns). Let them (risky) / Bar the gates (default) (a raid).
19. **A beaten raider pleads for mercy** after a raid. Spare them (a prisoner or a recruit) / Execute them (default) (renown).
20. **War drums in the distance**: an army is passing through. Hide in the walls (work stops a day) / Trade with them (default) (coins; some risk).
21. **A spy is caught** in the stores. Question them (the next raid's timing) / Hang them (default) / Let them go (a favour later).
22. **The watchman fell asleep** and raiders nearly got in. Punish them / Forgive them (default) (morale).
23. **A rival's relic** turns up in a traveller's pack. Buy it (coins; it may draw that lord) / Leave it (default).
24. **The Horde's riders** offer a horse trade. Trade (horses for stores) / Refuse (default).

## Plague, hunger and weather

25. **A fever spreads** in one home. Quarantine it (they may die; the rest are safe) / Nurse them together (default) (risk).
26. **The well is foul**. Dig a new one (work and stone) / Boil the water (default) (slower work a few days).
27. **Early frost** threatens the harvest. Harvest now (smaller yield) / Wait and hope (default) (all or nothing).
28. **Locusts** on the horizon. Burn the edge fields (lose some, save the rest) / Pray (default) (random).
29. **Flood**: the river rises. Save the fields / Save the stores (default) (one is damaged).
30. **Wildfire** in the forest. Cut a firebreak (lose some forest) / Let it burn out (default) (it may reach the town).
31. **A hard winter** is coming, the elders say. Ration food now (morale down, fewer deaths) / Eat as usual (default).
32. **Drought**: Carry water from the river (work) / Let the fields rest (default) (no harvest this season).
33. **A bumper crop**: Feast (morale) / Store it all (default) / Sell it to travellers (coins).
34. **Rats in the granary**. Get cats (need a trader) / Poison (default) (a small risk to people) / Burn the granary.
35. **A plague ship** (coast) asks to land. Let them (trade; plague risk) / Wave them off (default).
36. **Poisoned berries** made three people sick. Burn the bushes / Mark them and move on (default).

## Faith, omens and the strange

37. **A comet** in the sky. Hold a ritual (herbs; morale) / Ignore it (default) / Call it a bad sign (the town prepares: defence up, morale down).
38. **An eclipse**. Hide indoors (work stops) / Watch it (default) (research boost, a little fear).
39. **A holy wanderer** asks to build a shrine. Let them (morale building, costs stone) / Refuse (default).
40. **The dead walk** in the graveyard one night. Burn the graves (grief) / Post a guard (default) / Ask the necromancer (necromancer).
41. **A talking animal** at the edge of the woods (druid, fae). Follow it (an expedition) / Shoo it away (default).
42. **A fairy ring** appears in a field (fae, forest). Dance in it (random: blessing or curse) / Mow it down (default).
43. **A voice in the well** offers a wish. Wish for gold / Wish for health / Seal the well (default).
44. **A meteor falls** nearby. Dig it out (rare minerals; risky) / Leave it (default).
45. **Blood moon** (vampire, werewolf). Hold the hunt (fight boost; someone may be hurt) / Lock the doors (default).
46. **A phylactery's whisper** (lich). Feed it a soul (a townsperson; power) / Starve it (default) (the lich weakens a while).
47. **A ghost** haunts a home. Exorcise it (herbs) / Live with it (default) (morale down there) / Speak with it (a secret).
48. **Strange lights** over the marsh. Investigate (someone goes; could vanish) / Stay away (default).
49. **The founder dreams** of a place far away. Send scouts (a new destination) / Forget it (default).
50. **A statue** unearthed while digging. Set it up in the square (morale) / Sell it (default) (coins) / Smash it (a curse lifted, or not).

## Trade and coin

51. **A merchant offers a rare relic** for most of the coins. Buy it / Pass (default).
52. **A caravan's axle broke** outside town. Help them (work; a gift) / Charge them (default) (coins) / Ignore them.
53. **A moneylender** offers a loan. Borrow (coins now, more back later) / Refuse (default).
54. **Counterfeit coins** found in the till. Hunt the forger (a fight) / Swallow the loss (default).
55. **A noble wants to buy the town's best item**. Sell (lots of coins) / Keep it (default).
56. **A traveller's dog** won't leave. Keep it (a guard dog; eats) / Shoo it (default).
57. **The guild offers a contract**: send three people away for two days for a big reward. Send them / Decline (default).
58. **Tax collector** from a far kingdom (Medieval+). Pay / Refuse (default) (a small raid later) / Bribe them.
59. **A rich traveller dies** at the tavern. Keep the purse / Send it to their family (default) (renown).
60. **A market fair** asks to set up in town. Host it (coins, crowds, some theft) / Decline (default).
61. **Smugglers** want to store goods in the cellars. Let them (coins; raids more likely) / Refuse (default).
62. **A caravan offers exotic seeds**. Buy (a new crop grows faster for a season) / Pass (default).

## Townsfolk and their lives

63. **Two townsfolk quarrel** over a spouse, a debt, a tool. Side with one / Side with the other / Let them sort it out (default) (a fight may break out).
64. **A townsperson wants to leave** for the city. Let them go / Ask them to stay (default) (they may sulk).
65. **A marriage proposal** between townsfolk of rival jobs. Bless it / Forbid it (default) (both sulk).
66. **A thief among you**: stores keep going missing. Search the homes (morale down) / Set a trap (default) (a few days).
67. **An elder is dying** and asks to see the sea (or the mountains). Send them with an escort / Keep them comfortable (default).
68. **A child's prank** set a fire. Punish them / Laugh it off (default) (another prank later).
69. **The best hunter** wants to go after the beast that killed their brother. Let them (an expedition alone) / Forbid it (default).
70. **A duel for the founder's honour**. Accept (the founder or a champion fights) / Decline (default) (morale down a little).
71. **A townsperson confesses** to a past crime. Forgive / Exile them (default).
72. **A drunk brawl** at the tavern. Throw them in the stocks / Let it go (default).
73. **Someone wants to build their own home** away from town. Let them (a lone house, raids hit it first) / No (default).
74. **A townsperson has a talent** (music, carving). Give them time off to practise (a new ware later) / Keep them working (default).
75. **A funeral**: Hold a great wake (food; grief passes faster) / Bury them quietly (default).
76. **A birthday of the founder**. Hold a feast (food; morale) / Work as usual (default).
77. **An apprentice** wants to learn a trade from a master. Let them (skill grows fast, both slower at work) / Not now (default).
78. **The midwife** needs herbs no one has. Send someone to the hills (an expedition) / Make do (default).

## Land, ruins and beasts

79. **An old ruin** found nearby. Dig now (loot; it may wake something) / Seal it (default) / Study it (research).
80. **A monster's lair** found. Hunt it (a boss fight, a trophy) / Leave it be (default).
81. **A cave** opens after a quake. Explore (ore; danger) / Block it (default).
82. **Wolves** have been taking the livestock. Hunt the pack / Build a better fence (default) (wood).
83. **A bear** sleeps under the stockpile. Drive it off (a fight) / Let it sleep (default) (the pile is off limits a day).
84. **A giant tree** falls across the road. Cut it up (lots of wood; a day's work) / Go around it (default) (slower hauling).
85. **A spring of hot water** found. Build a bathhouse (morale; stone) / Leave it (default).
86. **A sinkhole** swallows a field. Fill it (stone) / Make it a pond (default) (fish).
87. **Wild horses** in the meadow. Catch them (horses; someone may get hurt) / Let them run (default).
88. **Bees** in the old oak. Take the honey (stings) / Leave them (default) (the fields grow a little better).
89. **A dragon** is seen over the mountains (rare). Hide the gold / Offer tribute / Ignore it (default) (maybe it comes).
90. **Bandit trail** found. Follow it (loot a hideout) / Report it to travellers (default) (renown).

## Era and origin moments

91. **The first printing press** (Medieval). Print pamphlets (research up, unrest) / Print scripture (default) (morale).
92. **Railway surveyors** want to lay a line through (Industrial). Allow it (travellers up, a field lost) / Refuse (default).
93. **A factory strike** (Industrial). Raise wages (coins) / Wait it out (default) (no crafting two days).
94. **A radio broadcast** calls for volunteers (Modern). Send them (renown, lose people a while) / Stay home (default).
95. **A drone crash** in the square (Space). Salvage it (circuits) / Return it (default) (coins).
96. **The machines ask for names** (robot). Name them (spirits up) / Keep serial numbers (default).
97. **The grove asks for a sacrifice** (druid). Give a tree-felling tool / Give nothing (default) (the forest grows back faster).
98. **The court demands a ball** (vampire). Hold it (coins spent; prices up a season) / Cancel it (default) (morale down).
99. **The pack wants to hunt the moon** (werewolf). Let them run (fight up; someone lost) / Chain the gates (default).
100. **The tribe splits over the route** (nomads, before settling). Go by the river (slow, safe) / Over the pass (default) (fast, raiders).
